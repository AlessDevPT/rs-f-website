The case

Spreadsheets get messy.
Bank-linked apps see too much.

Linking a finance app to your bank means handing your password to another company and trusting them to keep it safe. Finacy takes a different route.

The usual way

You hand your bank password to another company so it can pull your transactions in the background — every day, around the clock.

Your money information travels through other companies' computers you'll never see.

With Finacy

You download your monthly statement from your bank — the same file you've always been able to download — and open it in Finacy. That's it.

No password shared. No connection. Nothing leaves your phone.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Built to read the statements you give it, sort them automatically, and answer the questions that actually matter — without ever sending anything online.

Build your own reports

Your own little analytics studio, in your pocket.

Pick a chart type, choose what to group by, pick the measure, set the date range — Finacy redraws the chart for you. It's a tiny analytics workbench in your pocket, so you can answer the questions other finance apps don't even ask.

Add your own statements

Download a statement from your bank's website yourself and open it in Finacy — a CSV or a PDF both work. The app figures out the columns for you, and reads the tables straight out of a PDF; your bank password never touches the app.

Auto-Assign

Tell Finacy once to categorize "Lufthansa" as "Travel" — and from then on every flight files itself in the right place.

Clear insights

Where your money goes and how this month compares to the last. All worked out right on your phone.

Budget planning

Pin income, expense and savings lines. Finacy shows you the runway before the month begins.

Multiple profiles

Keep separate profiles for yourself, a partner or a side business — each with its own accounts, banks and history, all on one phone.

Locked with your phone

Your records stay inside Finacy on your phone, opened by the same PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face that unlocks the device. No extra password to remember.

Privacy model

The shortest path between
you and your money.

Most finance apps sit between you and your bank, quietly pulling your transactions through services you'll never see. Finacy doesn't. The picture on the right is the whole story.

  • No bank connection.

    We never see, store, or pass on your bank password — because we never ask for it.

  • Only on your phone.

    Your transactions, categories and rules live inside Finacy on your phone. Nothing uploads in the background. Nothing is tracked.

  • Secured by your phone lock.

    Your data is kept safe by your phone's built-in protection, and only opens with your PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face.

  • Backups stay yours.

    Save a protected backup to your phone whenever you like. Nothing is uploaded for you.
