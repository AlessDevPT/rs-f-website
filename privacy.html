Privacy Policy

App: Finacy · Last updated: 26 May 2026

Finacy is a local-first personal finance app. Your transactions, budgets, plans, asset balances, profile name and avatar live in a database on your device. We do not operate servers that store your data.

What we collect

  • On-device data you enter or import: transactions, categories, rules, monthly plan, asset accounts, saved reports, settings, your display name (max 12 characters), and an optional profile photo. All stored locally; we never see it.
  • Subscription data processed by RevenueCat and the platform store (Google Play Billing / Apple App Store) when you subscribe or restore: an anonymous app-user ID, the product purchased, and the store receipt. We use this only to unlock or restore Premium.
  • Font requests to Google's font CDN (via the google_fonts package). Cached on device after first load.

We do not collect: contacts, calendar, photos beyond the avatar you pick, SMS, call logs, browsing history, location, advertising ID, or any device identifier. We do not run analytics, telemetry, advertising, push notifications, or crash reporting.

What we do with it

  • Run the App on your device (dashboards, plans, charts).
  • Process subscriptions through RevenueCat and the platform store.
  • Personalise the experience (greeting by name, pre-filled onboarding answers).

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising or training. The only third parties that see anything are the ones listed below, strictly for their stated purpose.

Your choices

Because data lives on your device, you can:

  • Edit anything from its respective screen.
  • Delete per item from the same screens, or wipe everything via Profile → Reset all local data (irreversible).
  • Uninstall the App — Android / iOS removes its private storage.
  • Import a sample profile JSON to evaluate the App.
  • Export your profile JSON (Premium subscribers only) — Profile → Export profile.
  • Manage your subscription via More → Subscriptions → Manage in Google Play.

If you live in a region with statutory rights (EU/EEA, UK, California, …), you can exercise those rights directly in-app as above. For requests we cannot fulfil ourselves — e.g. subscription data held by RevenueCat — email us at finacyapp@gmail.com.

Security

  • The App's database lives in OS-private storage, sandboxed from other apps.
  • An optional biometric / device-credential App Lock is available in settings.
  • There is no central account to breach because there is no cloud account.
